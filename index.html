Hi, I'm Buket Melisa Evin.

I am a UX researcher and designer with backgrounds in Artificial Intelligence Engineering and Cyberpsychology. My work combines research, psychology and technical understanding to explore how people interact with digital products and AI systems.

I enjoy identifying where an experience creates unnecessary effort and translating those findings into clear, accessible design decisions. I am particularly interested in human-centred AI, accessibility, healthcare technology and interaction design.

How I Work

Design Guided by Research

I begin by understanding user needs, behaviours and existing pain points before deciding what should change. Familiar patterns that already work are preserved, while research guides where improvement can create meaningful value.

01

Understand

Explore user goals, behaviours, pain points and the existing product experience.

02

Define

Translate findings into focused problems, priorities and measurable design goals.

03

Design

Build solutions around established mental models, accessibility and human-centred interaction principles.

04

Evaluate

Test the experience, identify remaining friction and iterate rather than treating a design as finished.
